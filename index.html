Our edge · powered by Libre Computer

Hardware codecs, straight to HDMI

Our strongest advantage runs under the hood. Courtesy of Libre Computer, dedicated silicon decodes high-bitrate 4K and 8K HDR video in hardware and drives it directly out advanced HDMI — flawlessly and around the clock, keeping a wall of displays frame-synced in lockstep at up to 240 Hz, at a fraction of the power and cost of media-player PCs. It is the engine behind every display, sign, and stream we run.
